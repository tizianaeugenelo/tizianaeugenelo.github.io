Dvoretzky's theorem2026
Research project, Galilean School · Supervisor: Alessandro Pigati (Bocconi University)

A self-contained account of the local theory of Banach spaces needed for Dvoretzky's theorem: the Banach–Mazur compactum, John's and Lewis' theorems, and Gaussian measures. It then presents Pisier's Gaussian form of Milman's concentration-of-measure proof, with the sharp bound n ~ log N.

Seminar talk Galileian School, september 22nd 2026.
