Stability and curvature estimates after Schoen–Simon–Yau and Fischer-Colbrie–Schoen, the stable Bernstein problem in low dimensions (Chodosh–Li), and recent work of Bellettini.

Seminar talkupcoming University of Padova, late October 2026.

Dvoretzky's theorem2026
Research project, Galilean School · Supervisor: Alessandro Pigati (Bocconi University)

A self-contained account of the local theory of Banach spaces needed for Dvoretzky's theorem: the Banach–Mazur compactum, John's and Lewis' theorems, and Gaussian measures. It then presents Pisier's Gaussian form of Milman's concentration-of-measure proof, with the sharp bound n ~ log N.

Report (PDF)
Hausdorff measure of critical values2025
Bachelor thesis, University of Padova · Supervisor: Davide Vittone

Sard's 1965 theorem on the Hausdorff measure of the set of critical values, in the setting of Banach manifolds: a sharp refinement of the classical Morse–Sard theorem.
